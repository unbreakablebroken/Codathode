01 / ARCHITECTURE

100% Local Browser Execution

At Codathode, we believe user privacy is an architectural requirement, not an optional marketing bullet point. Every single utility provided on this site—including the QR Code Generator, Password Generator, JSON Formatter, Base64 Converter, Text Diff Checker, and Grade Calculators—operates entirely within your local device's web browser.

Key Architecture Rule: When you input passwords, text, URLs, JSON payloads, or uploaded images, those inputs never leave your computer or mobile device. They are processed using client-side JavaScript in your device's temporary memory and are immediately cleared when you close or refresh the page.
02 / DATA COLLECTION

Information We Do Not Collect

Because Codathode operates with zero backend data storage, we do not collect, store, sell, or share:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Payload Data: No text, files, images, or strings entered into any tool.
  • Keystroke Telemetry: No analytics recording your typing velocity or interactions.
  • User Accounts: No registration, login, or credentials exist on our platform.
03 / THIRD PARTIES & ADVERTISING

Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

Codathode does not set any first-party tracking cookies or employ persistent session fingerprinting.

To keep this suite completely free for all users worldwide without subscriptions or paywalls, third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may be utilized in the future to display advertisements. These third-party vendors may use standard web cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites across the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice tool.

04 / ASSETS & CDNS

Third-Party Content Delivery Networks

To deliver rapid load times and font rendering, Codathode references static assets from trusted public content delivery networks (Google Fonts and Cloudflare CDN for specific parsing utilities like qrcode.js and marked.js). These requests are standard HTTP requests strictly limited to fetching public script and font files.
