CODATHODE / MANIFESTO

BUILT WITH
INTENTION.

Codathode was founded on a simple conviction: modern digital utilities shouldn't spy on you, bloat your device, or demand your personal information. It is a curated collection of fast, browser-local instruments engineered with deliberate restraint.

ORIGIN & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Crafted by a Student Developer in India

Codathode was envisioned, designed, and built by a student developer in India who grew tired of everyday web tools cluttered with intrusive tracking pixels, popups, paywalls, and heavy single-page-app dependencies.

Whether you are converting strings, inspecting colors, calculating examination grades, or hashing Base64 strings, you deserve software that gets out of your way and executes with uncompromising speed.

TECHNICAL INTEGRITY

Pure Vanilla. Zero Telemetry.

Every tool on Codathode runs 100% locally in your web browser using pure, unadulterated HTML5, CSS3, and standard Web APIs. No megabytes of JavaScript frameworks. No analytical beacons.

Your data never traverses our servers because we operate no backend data ingestion. The code executes in your machine's volatile memory and is purged the moment you close the tab.

Our Governing Principles

NON-NEGOTIABLE STANDARDS
